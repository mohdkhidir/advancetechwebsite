Three specialist systems — each engineered by Barkey for a specific clinical application, backed by decades of German precision manufacturing.

MDR Class B · MDA Registered
plasmatherm
· Germany
"Water-free plasma thawing. Zero contamination risk."

The Barkey plasmatherm is the clinical gold standard for thawing Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP), cryoprecipitate, and cryopreserved stem cell therapies. Its exclusive dry-heat technology eliminates any direct water contact with blood bags — protecting patients and staff from cross-contamination in blood banks, transfusion units, and cell therapy departments.

Key Advantages
Thaws up to 4 bags simultaneously (emergency: up to 8)
Closed water circuit — no bag-to-water exposure
3 safety layers: electronic + mechanical overtemp + humidity sensor
Transparent lid for continuous visual monitoring
Water change required only once per year
Customizable programs for plasma, blood, and HPC therapies
Temperature Range+37°C to +45°C
Capacity4–8 bags

MDR Class C · MDA Registered
S-line
· Germany
"Inline warming. Patient-ready in seconds."

The Barkey S-line is a next-generation inline blood and infusion fluid warmer designed for transfusion, IV therapy, and dialysis. Its patented silicone warming sleeve with embedded heating wires delivers rapid, uniform warming precisely at the point of delivery — eliminating the risk of hypothermia during large-volume fluid administration.

Key Advantages
Precise 39°C delivery — measured at the patient end of the line
Flow rates up to 25 ml/min for high-volume therapy
Single-touch on/off — intuitive operation for nursing staff
No annual safety check required — reducing maintenance burden
Wide voltage range (100–240 VAC) — compatible with any ward
Available in 1.2 m and 1.5 m sleeve lengths
Set Temperature39°C (fixed, clinically validated)
Max Flow Rate25 ml/min
Power Supply100–240 VAC · 50/60 Hz
MDA Reg. No.GC3201324-164319

Medical Equipment
warming center II
· Germany
"Always ready. Perfectly conditioned."

The Barkey warming center II is a professional double-drawer warming cabinet that keeps IV fluids, blood products, and clinical textiles at precise therapeutic temperatures — ensuring they are immediately ready when seconds matter. Its circulating-air filtration system actively removes contaminants, maintaining a hygienic environment for every stored product.

Key Advantages
Dual-zone: +35–40°C for fluids / +41–55°C for textiles
Capacity: 40 × 1-litre infusion bottles (two drawers)
HEPA-filtered circulating air — contamination-free storage
Electronic + mechanical overtemperature alarm
Open-drawer alarm after 1 minute — prevents temperature drift
Ultra-low standby power (<1.5 W) — energy efficient
